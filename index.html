About us

Our mission

Our mission is to create a safe space for teens to get away from academics and technology and just hang out. With so much academic stress and so much technology in everyday life, teens need a place to unwind and relax.

🏀

Physical interaction

Face-to-face time in a world that is mostly through screens.

🌱

Social growth

Meet new people, practice teamwork, and build confidence.

☀️

Stepping away from academics

A place where being a student comes second to being a teenager.

💪

Exercise is good for you

Movement lowers stress and boosts mood, and here it feels like play.
